How to Make Press-Ons Last

Good prep and gentle habits make a big difference to wear time.

Avoid soaking your hands immediately after application, use gloves for prolonged water or cleaning exposure and do not use your nails as tools. When removing, soften the adhesive first and never force a nail off.

Keep it simple

The exact method should always follow the instructions supplied with your YK set and adhesive. If you are unsure about sizing or application, contact us before ordering.
