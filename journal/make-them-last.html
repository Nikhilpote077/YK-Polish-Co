Make them last longer

Simple habits that protect your sets.

While wearing

  • Avoid using nails as tools
  • Wear gloves for long water exposure
  • Reapply thin top coat if shine dulls

Removal

  • Soak in warm soapy water or use cuticle oil
  • Gently lift — never force
  • Remove residual glue with oil

Storage & reuse

  • Clean and store in the original case
  • Many sets can be worn more than once
