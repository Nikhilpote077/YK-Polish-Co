How to Measure Your Nails

Before you order a full set, spend a few minutes getting your measurements right.

Start with clean, dry nails and measure the widest point of each nail. Record thumb through pinky for both hands. If a nail sits between two sizes, choose the larger option and confirm against the brand chart.

Keep it simple

The exact method should always follow the instructions supplied with your YK set and adhesive. If you are unsure about sizing or application, contact us before ordering.
