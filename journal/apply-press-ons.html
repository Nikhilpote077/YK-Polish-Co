How to Apply Press-On Nails

A clean application begins before the nail ever touches your natural nail.

Push back the cuticle gently, remove surface oils, lightly buff if appropriate and wipe the nail clean. Choose the correct size, apply adhesive according to the product instructions and hold the press-on firmly for the recommended time.

Keep it simple

The exact method should always follow the instructions supplied with your YK set and adhesive. If you are unsure about sizing or application, contact us before ordering.
