How to apply press-ons

A clean prep and firm press make all the difference.

Prep your nails

Wash hands, push back cuticles, lightly buff, wipe with alcohol.

Select sizes

Match each tip edge-to-edge without side overhang.

Apply adhesive

Use glue or tabs provided. Thin layer; wait a few seconds for glue.

Press and hold

Place from cuticle, press firmly 20–30 seconds. Avoid water for 1 hour.
